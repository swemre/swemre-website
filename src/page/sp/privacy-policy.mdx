This policy explains what personal data the website at swemre.com ("the site") handles, why, and what choices you have. The site is operated by Emre Caniklioglu ("swemre", "I", "me"), who is the data controller. The site is a personal portfolio and does not sell data or show advertising.

## Who is responsible

Emre Caniklioglu, reachable at emre@swemre.com. Write to this address for any question about this policy or your data.

## What the site handles

### Resume request form

If you request my resume you provide your full name, email address and the reason for your request. I use it only to review and answer your request. Legal basis: my legitimate interest in replying to people who contact me (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and steps you ask me to take (Art. 6(1)(b)).

At the time of writing the form is not yet connected to a server. Your submission is held in the memory of your own browser tab and is discarded when you close or reload the page. When a server is connected, this policy will be updated before it goes live.

### Preferences stored in your browser

The site stores a few values in your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device and are not used to identify you:

- your chosen colour theme and whether sound effects are muted;
- which articles you opened, liked or saved, and how often you viewed them;
- the conversations you write in the chat widget. Chat is not yet connected to a server, so these messages stay on your device and do not reach me.

You can remove them at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

### Spotify content

The "Songs I liked" list shows album artwork loaded from Spotify's image servers, which receive your IP address when the images load. When you press play, the site loads Spotify's embedded player from open.spotify.com. From that moment Spotify processes data, and may set cookies, under its own privacy policy. Nothing from Spotify loads until you press play, except the artwork.

### Technical data

Whoever hosts the site may process technical data such as your IP address and request time in server logs to keep the service secure and running. The site itself runs no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

## Links to other sites

The site links to external services such as GitHub and LinkedIn. They have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site. Telephone and email links open your own phone or email application.

## Retention

Data from the resume form is kept only as long as needed to answer your request, and no longer than twelve months afterwards unless a longer period is required by law. Browser preferences stay on your device until you delete them.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, and you may object to processing. Email emre@swemre.com and I will respond within one month. You may also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

## Security

I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data I handle. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
